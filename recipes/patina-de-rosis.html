Patina de Rosis (Rose Brain & Fish Sauce Egg Custard Pie)

Description

This classic Roman custard recipe originates from De Re Coquinaria, the iconic ancient cookbook compiled by gourmet Marcus Gavius Apicius, popular among Roman nobles in the 5th century. The term patina refers to crustless baked egg custards cooked over charcoal embers traditionally. It blends creamy tender brain mousse, fragrant edible rose, umami-rich fermented fish sauce (liquamen/Garum) and sweet dessert wine, adopting the signature Roman sweet-savory flavor pairing. The Bilibili creator reproduced the ancient charcoal heating method with modern water-bath baking for home cooking. No extra salt is needed, as fish sauce provides all salty notes.

Ingredients

  • 2 sets pig/calf brains (≈400g)
  • 4 whole eggs
  • 80ml liquamen (ancient Roman fermented fish sauce; Thai/Vietnamese fish sauce for home substitute)
  • 120ml passum (ancient raisin sweet wine; blend sweet red wine + honey as replacement)
  • 80ml edible rose water / fresh damask rose petals
  • 5g freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Steps

  1. Brain deodorization & pre-processing

    Soak brains in cold water for 60 minutes, pick off all fascia and blood clots with toothpicks, rinse clean. Simmer at low gentle boil for 10 minutes until brains turn solid gray and fork can pierce easily. Drain water, mash finely and sift for smoother texture.

  2. Prepare rose flavor base

    Trim bitter white root sections off fresh rose petals, crush petals with partial fish sauce, strain to collect rose juice and discard residues. Mix the juice evenly with brain puree.

  3. Blend egg-wine batter

    Whisk eggs fully, stir in remaining fish sauce, sweet wine, rose water, olive oil and black pepper. Pour the liquid into rose brain puree in batches, stir thoroughly to eliminate lumps.

  4. Water-bath baking

    Grease an oven-safe bowl with olive oil, pour in the batter. Place the bowl inside a larger baking tray, add warm water to half the bowl height. Preheat oven to 180℃, bake 40–45 minutes. The dish is done if a fork inserted at the center pulls out clean with runny egg residue.

  5. Finish & serve

    Rest 5 minutes after baking, garnish with dried rose petals and extra black pepper directly.

Cooking Tips

  • Only use food-grade edible roses, ornamental roses are inedible.
  • Skip added salt entirely to avoid over-seasoning from fish sauce.
