Porcellum Hortulanum (Garden Stuffed Suckling Pig, Pig-Chicken Integrated Roast)

Description

Also sourced from Apicius' ancient cookbook, this luxurious banquet staple was exclusive to upper-class Roman feasts. Cooks debone a whole suckling pig from the throat to keep intact pig skin as a hollow pouch, stuff it with minced chicken, sausages, fruits, garden greens and nuts, then slow roast whole. The Bilibili creator simplified rare ancient ingredients (wild mallows, thrushes etc.) for home preparation, retaining the classic Roman sweet-savory glaze made with honey, sweet wine and fish sauce.

Ingredients

Main body

  • 1 whole suckling pig (fully boned via neck incision, hollow skin reserved)

Chicken mixed stuffing

  • 350g minced boneless chicken
  • cooked chopped pig offal (kidney/liver)
  • 2 sliced Lucanian sausages
  • 6 pitted chopped dates
  • 1 leek
  • celery
  • onion
  • spinach (sub for ancient mallow leaves)
  • beetroot
  • 100g ground almonds
  • 4 eggs
  • liquamen
  • freshly ground black pepper

Glazing sauce

  • 250ml pork/chicken stock
  • sweet wine (passum)
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • rosemary (sub for ancient rue herb)
  • olive oil
  • starch slurry for light thickening

Steps

  1. Prep whole suckling pig

    Remove residual blood inside the pig, rub inner and outer surfaces evenly with olive oil, fish sauce and black pepper, marinate for 30 minutes.

  2. Mix stuffing

    Combine minced chicken, chopped offal, sausage slices, dates, diced vegetables, almond crumbs and eggs. Season with fish sauce and pepper (no extra salt added).

  3. Stuff and roast

    Fill the pig cavity tightly with stuffing, stitch the neck opening closed tightly. Preheat oven to 170℃, roast for 3.5 hours. Baste pig skin repeatedly with olive oil + sweet wine mixture during roasting to prevent drying and cracking.

  4. Simmer finishing glaze

    Boil meat stock, sweet wine, honey, rosemary and pepper over low heat, thicken lightly with starch slurry. Slice open the roasted pig along its back, drizzle warm glaze before serving.

Cooking Tips

  • Home alternative: replace whole suckling pig with hollowed pork belly stuffed with chicken filling for easier handling.
  • Rosemary perfectly replicates the herbal aroma of hard-to-source ancient rue.
